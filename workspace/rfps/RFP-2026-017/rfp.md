# RFP-2026-017 — Supply of 40 CNC vertical machining centres

**Issuer:** Contoso Industrial Systems B.V., Rotterdam plant
**Status:** Open — bids received, under evaluation
**Evaluation currency:** EUR
**Category:** Machine Tools

## 1. Scope
Supply, delivery (DAP Rotterdam), installation and commissioning of forty (40) three-axis CNC vertical machining centres
for the Rotterdam plant expansion.

## 2. Minimum technical requirements
- Table size at least 1,000 × 500 mm, X/Y/Z travel at least 1,000 / 500 / 500 mm
- Spindle 12,000 rpm or faster, BT40 or HSK-A63
- Automatic tool changer with at least 30 positions
- Controller with Ethernet/OPC-UA connectivity for the Contoso MES
- CE marking; supplier certified to ISO 9001

## 3. Commercial requirements
- Unit price per machine including installation and commissioning
- Delivery lead time from purchase order, in weeks
- Warranty period in months (parts and labour)
- Bids in currencies other than EUR are converted at Contoso's fixed rates

## 4. Evaluation
See `criteria.md`. Vendors on Contoso's restricted-parties list are ineligible.

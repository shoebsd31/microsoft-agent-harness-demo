# RFP-2026-017 — Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| Price | 35 | Unit price in EUR; the cheapest compliant-data bid scores 100 |
| Delivery lead time | 20 | Weeks from purchase order; the shortest scores 100 |
| Warranty | 15 | Months; the longest scores 100 |
| Technical compliance | 20 | Percentage of the specification met (from the technical review) |
| Sustainability | 10 | 100 points if the vendor holds ISO 14001 |

Weighted total = Σ(weight × score) / 100. Required certifications: ISO 9001, CE.
Ambiguous commercial terms must be clarified with the vendor before award.
